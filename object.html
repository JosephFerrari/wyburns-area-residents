There are plans to build 94 houses and flats on Lower Wyburns Farm. This would mean potentially hundreds more cars using Daws Heath Road and worsening traffic on the Rayleigh Weir and A127. It would also increase stress on Rayleigh's already overburdened public services, such as schools and GPs. We want to fight these plans, but we need your help. Please keep reading for a step by step guide on how to participate.

The last day for objections is
Sunday August 6th
Please submit by the end of this date.

We are fighting on two fronts. Firstly, a report has been drafted on behalf of Rochford Council that proposes Lower Wyburns Farm should be reclassified from Green Belt to Grey Belt. Secondly, a planning application has been submitted by Bellway to build 94 houses and flats on Lower Wyburns Farm. We need you to submit objections to both of these proposals.
