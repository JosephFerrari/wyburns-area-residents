Rochford Council has commissioned a consultancy firm to carry out an assessment of all green belt land in the district, splitting it into parcels and proposing which should be designated as grey belt. It can be found here:

Rochford Green Belt Assessment 2026 (Draft)

Lower Wyburns Farm has been put forward as potential grey belt, and should that decision be solidified, it would bolster any plans to build over that land.

The decision will be made in a council meeting taking place on Thursday the 27th of August, and we have until then to submit our reasons for objecting to the proposal.
