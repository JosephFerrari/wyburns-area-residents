94 houses
are being proposed on
Lower Wyburns Farm,
Daws Heath Road.


We believe the plans are
unsustainable
and we need your help
to challenge them.



Why this is important:

  • Already congested areas, such as the Rayleigh Weir and recycling centre on Castle Road, will be worsened by the increased traffic.

  • Already crowded public services, such as schools and GP surgeries, will be further strained by the increased population.

  • Our valuable greenbelt will continue to shrink, irreversibly.
