How to object to the Grey Belt proposal

We have researched and cultivated high quality, evidence backed objections to the Green Belt Assessment Draft which we are hosting here. If you agree with our conclusion that Lower Wyburns Farm should not be reclassified as Grey Belt, please read and then complete the following steps:

  1. Enter your name and postcode into the boxes below.

  2. Press the “CREATE EMAIL” button.

  3. If asked, specify which email app you would like to use.

  4. If the email has successfully been pre-populated with recipient addresses, a subject line, and a message body, press send.

If step 4 fails, please retry the steps.
